# Công viên Tượng đài Chiến thắng Long Khánh 3D

A walkable, third-person 3D model of the Victory Monument park in Long Khánh, Đồng Nai, Vietnam. It runs in the browser (three.js r128); open `index.html` through a web server or GitHub Pages.

This is a hand-built approximation made from reference photos. It is not a scan or a survey, and the surrounding shophouses, signs, food carts and people are fictional.

Created by **Mike Do X Claude**.

## Controls

- Move: WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on phones. Camera: drag. Jump: **Space**, or **Nhảy** on phones.
- Interact: **E** (or the yellow button). At a table with food in your bag, **F** or the blue button: *Ngồi ăn / Sit & Eat*. Press E again to stand.
- **J** or 📒: side-quest journal. 🎆: fireworks events (Start, Stop, Replay; sound off by default). 👁 or **H**: hide the HUD (E and Space still work).
- Esc closes the topmost dialog; every card has × and –.

## Gameplay additions

Traffic signals and the traffic officer at the Hùng Vương crossing, the fireworks shows, the side quests and their props, and the seating/eating system are game features, not features of the real park. See `RESEARCH.md` and `STOREFRONT_PLACEMENTS.json` for what is and isn't verified.
