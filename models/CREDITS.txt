City bundle (skies, shophouses, scooters) - added 15 Nov 2026
Skies: three Poly Haven HDRIs, CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
  Kloppenheim 03 (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_03_puresky - day
  Industrial Sunset (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_sunset_puresky - sunset
  Kloppenheim 01 (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_01_puresky - overcast sunset alternative
  Changes: the files in skies/ are 2K and 1K derivatives (the day sky was reduced from 4K), rolled horizontally so the
  photographed sun matches the scene's sun direction. Radiance values are otherwise unchanged. The unmodified originals are
  kept with the project sources, not on the page. There is no photographic night sky; the night is procedural.
Shophouses (models/buildings): four original stylised Vietnamese-inspired models generated for this project
  (source/create_shophouses.py in the bundle). Not downloaded third-party models, not surveyed buildings in Long Khanh.
  Changes: repacked as glTF JSON; the unaccented mesh lettering is not drawn and is replaced by accented generic signs
  (CÀ PHÊ, TẠP HÓA, BÁNH MÌ ...) drawn by the page; widths scaled 0.85-1.25 to fit the plots; plaster tint varied slightly.
  The Sketchfab models listed in the bundle's MORE_MODEL_DOWNLOADS.md were NOT included in the bundle and are NOT used.
Scooters (models/scooters), all CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/):
  "low poly scooter" by Thomas Saint Pierre (s1pierro), https://poly.pizza/m/awXCP7LUcz6
  "Scooter" by Poly by Google, https://poly.pizza/m/eHdEFPwUfCt
  "Scooter" by MiniPoly, https://poly.pizza/m/3vLV1QtLFP
  Changes: repacked from GLB to glTF JSON (textures as PNG); uniformly scaled to 1.85 m length; a stray default cube
  in the s1pierro file is not drawn; MiniPoly emissive strength halved. Meshes are otherwise unchanged. Parked only.
See models/CITY_BUNDLE_LICENSES.txt and skies/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json.

Added 15 Nov 2026 (evening): scooter riders, food carts, Christmas lights, park lamps, rain and fog are all generated by the
